# Notices

TheFatCat is a memecoin protocol. Nothing in this documentation is financial,
investment, legal or tax advice, and nothing is a promise of profit.

Rewards depend on trading volume, the Belly balance, eligible position weight,
execution conditions and each position's selected asset. All digital assets
carry risk, including total loss. Read the mechanism, verified contracts and
current risk disclosures before interacting with the protocol.

This documentation may change before launch. Deployed and verified contract
code takes precedence over explanatory prose if the two ever disagree.
