# Staking & Positions FAQ

---

## 1. Why is there no published APY or APR?
Rewards vary with trading-tax revenue, the Belly's available balance, active position weights and execution conditions. The protocol makes no fixed-yield commitment.

---

## 2. What happens if I redeem my principal?
You may redeem your position at any time to withdraw 100% of your principal. There is zero exit fee or lockup penalty. The only trade-off is that redeeming forfeits the single currently open interval and ends that position's seniority. Any rewards settled in previous intervals remain permanently yours to claim in the distributor.

---

## 3. Why can't positions be merged or added to?
Each stake has its own seniority clock. Additional principal must open a new position and build its own seniority; it cannot inherit an existing position's multiplier. One wallet can manage multiple positions.

---

## 4. What is the minimum staking threshold?
Ordinary positions require at least 100,000 FATCAT, limiting small positions.
