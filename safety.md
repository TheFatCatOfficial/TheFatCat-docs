# Safety and status

The public safety model separates principal custody from the authority that
spends released reward capital. The design assumes that execution logic or an
operator key can eventually fail and limits the amount that can leave the Belly
within one window.

The child pages state the intended invariants and the current pre-launch risks.
They are requirements, not a substitute for deployed-code verification or an
independent review.
