# Background & Philosophy

Why TheFatCat was designed this way, the mathematical flaws of traditional dividend tokens, and what the protocol deliberately refuses to promise.

---

## Chapter Overview

Explore the fundamental architectural pillars and design philosophy of TheFatCat:

- **[Paradigm Shift & Dilemmas]({% link background/paradigm-shift.md %})**: An examination of the triple structural vulnerabilities in traditional reflection tokens (single-asset monopoly, volatility spikes, and flash-loan dilution).
- **[The Reservoir Model]({% link background/reservoir-model.md %})**: How The Belly transforms transient fee volume into a viscous, damped stock with discrete $1/21$ meal releases and multi-week shock absorption.
- **[Core Principles & Non-Goals]({% link background/principles.md %})**: Explicit boundaries of the protocol—no fixed yield promises, unpausable redemptions, linear capital scaling, and rejection of perpetual motion claims.
