# Background & Philosophy

Why TheFatCat was designed this way, the mathematical flaws of traditional dividend tokens, comparative mechanisms across benchmark DeFi protocols, the time-seniority mechanics, and what the protocol deliberately refuses to promise.

---

## Chapter Overview

Explore the fundamental architectural pillars and design philosophy of TheFatCat:

- **[Paradigm Shift & Dilemmas]({% link background/paradigm-shift.md %})**: A quantitative examination of the triple structural vulnerabilities in traditional reflection tokens (single-asset monopoly, volatility spikes, and hot-capital dilution).
- **[Prior Art & Comparative Matrix]({% link background/prior-art.md %})**: An exhaustive comparative matrix benchmarking TheFatCat against Curve Finance, Pendle V2, and Synthetix V3 across accounting, rounding, and rate-limiting dimensions.
- **[The Reservoir Model]({% link background/reservoir-model.md %})**: How The Belly transforms transient fee volume into a viscous, damped stock with discrete $1/21$ meal releases and multi-week shock absorption.
- **[The Power of Staked Time]({% link background/time-weighting.md %})**: Dismantling the TVL-only monopoly by introducing staked time as the core innovation variable—additive linear ramps (1.0× → 22.0×), natural defense moats, and ERC-721 duration crystallization.
- **[Core Principles & Non-Goals]({% link background/principles.md %})**: The protocol's three foundational design principles (The Belly reservoir damping, capital sovereignty & diet autonomy, the power of staked time) alongside four explicit Non-Goals.
