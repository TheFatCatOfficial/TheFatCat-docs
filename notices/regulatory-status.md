# Regulatory Status & Legal Jurisdiction

---

## 1. Code is Law & Precedence
Smart contracts on the blockchain are autonomous and irreversible:
1. **Precedence**: In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the explanatory prose in this documentation and the deployed on-chain bytecode, the **verified smart contract code on BNB Chain always takes absolute precedence**.
2. **Third-Party Venue Dependencies**: The protocol interacts with third-party decentralized applications and launchpads (including Flap and PancakeSwap). TheFatCat core contributors have no control over third-party infrastructure, RPC availability, or external token smart contracts.

---

## 2. Jurisdiction & User Responsibility
Users are solely responsible for complying with all applicable laws, regulations, and tax obligations in their relevant jurisdiction before acquiring FATCAT tokens or interacting with the protocol contracts. Access may be restricted or prohibited in jurisdictions under comprehensive sanctions or where decentralized digital asset staking is prohibited by law.
